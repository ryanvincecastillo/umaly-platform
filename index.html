How Umaly Works

From farmer to factory in five intelligent steps

AI Detects Low Stock

The Demand Planner continuously monitors inventory levels. When stock falls below 7 days, it triggers an alert and recommends creating a purchase order.

1

Harvest Scout Activates

The AI agent sends personalized SMS messages to farmers in their local language, considering weather conditions and communicating demand urgency.

2

Farmers Respond

Farmers reply with harvest quantities via SMS or app. AI aggregates forecasts and calculates Available-to-Promise inventory.

3

Station Commits

Buying station operators review ATP and propose realistic commitments to SMEs. Physical inventory + incoming forecasts = confident promises.

4

Quality Check & Dispatch

AI-powered grading analyzes crop quality at the station. Variance Auditor monitors weight discrepancies during transit to detect fraud.
